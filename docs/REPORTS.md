# Reporting Catalogue — v0.2.0

## Compliance Reports
- Daily compliance — single part or all parts.
- Weekly compliance — single part or all parts.
- Monthly compliance — single part or all parts.
- Metrics: Quantity, Sales ₹ and Tonnage MT.
- Comparisons: Plant, Customer, Type / Product Group, Part.
- Revised schedule requirements are used from their effective date.

## Management Reports
### Process & WIP
- Operation daily / weekly / monthly compliance.
- Operation comparison / bottleneck ranking.
- Single-part process funnel with Plan, Actual and WIP.

### Vendor
- Vendor receipt compliance (Received ÷ Sent).
- On-time return compliance.
- Pending WIP and overdue movement count.
- Vendor aging and monthly Sent vs Received trend.

### OEE & Loss
- OEE, Availability, Performance and Quality daily trend.
- Machine-wise OEE comparison.
- Loss Pareto by loss category.
- Machine loss-hours ranking.
- Monthly loss-hours trend.

### Actions
- Monthly actions raised vs closed.
- Closure compliance.
- Owner-wise open / overdue / aging report.
- Recurring problem-category Pareto.

### Schedule Impact
- Every schedule revision with previous target, new target and effective date.
- Quantity delta and estimated Sales ₹ impact.
- New average daily requirement after the revision.

### Month-end Forecast
- Product-wise target, actual, current run rate and projected month end.
- Recovery required per remaining working day.
- Risk: ON TRACK / WATCH / CRITICAL.
- Projected Sales ₹ gap.

## Exports
- Management report tables can be exported as CSV for Excel.
- Use `Print / Save PDF` to create a PDF snapshot of the selected report page.
